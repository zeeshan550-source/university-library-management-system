# Project Risk Analysis

## Introduction
Risk analysis helps identify possible problems that may affect the project.

## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internet Failure | Medium | High | Keep offline backups |
| Data Loss | Low | High | Maintain regular backups |
| Project Delay | Medium | High | Follow project schedule |
| Team Member Absence | Medium | Medium | Distribute responsibilities |
| Git Errors | Medium | Medium | Learn Git commands |

## Conclusion
Risk management helps the team identify problems early and complete the project successfully.
